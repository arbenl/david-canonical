## 1. CRITICAL VULNERABILITIES

### C1. Gate statistics were redefined from worst-case to average-case; code comments record that the stricter forms failed (A′ and B′)

This is the single most serious finding. The audit makes no claim about intent: what is verifiable is that (i) the implemented gate statistics are anti-conservative relative to the pre-registered theorem forms, and (ii) the in-line comments explicitly state that the stricter forms caused gate failures before the change. That combination is sufficient to treat the affected routes as unaudited, whatever the motivation for the edits.

**C1a — Theorem A′ gate (`fit.py::_run_theorem_gates`).** The thesis defines d(θ) = min(J₍₃₎, min(φ_g, 1−φ_g)) **per stratum g**, with φ_g the stratum prevalence. The implementation computes

φ_d = mean over all (regime, tactic) cells of logit⁻¹(α_activity)

with the in-line comment: *"Mean avoids the A′ gate failing because ONE near-zero cell (phi≈0) drags the identification distance to near 0."* The stale comment two lines above still says "Use min over (L, K) — worst-case". Mathematically, the class-balance term min(φ, 1−φ) exists precisely because a near-zero-prevalence cell is practically non-identified (Theorem A′, Operational Diagnostic). Averaging across cells lets unidentifiable cells ride through FG2 on the back of identified ones. **The gate, as implemented, does not test the theorem's condition.**

**C1b — Theorem B′ gate (`fit.py`).** FG3 condition 1 requires the 2.5% posterior quantile of I(O) per cell. The implementation computes `I_d.mean(axis=1)` — the **across-source mean** of |ρ_s − δ_s| — with the comment: *"min(axis=1) caused B′ to fail because one posterior tail with near-zero j dragged the lower 95% bound to 0.03... Mean gives the per-draw average source quality."* The variable is still named `I_worst`. A stratum with one informative source and several noise sources passes a gate whose theorem (B′.2, single-channel Fisher information) gives those noise sources **zero** information. There is no theorem under which the mean of Youden indices is the right aggregate.

**C1c — Theorem D′ gate (`fit.py`).** h\* is computed per initial regime with a **one-hot known-state** z_t, then averaged under the stationary weights: h\*_overall = round(Σ_r π_∞[r]·h\*_r). The thesis defines h\* per forecast origin via the filtered posterior ζ_T = P(Z_T | D_{1:T}). Stationary averaging (i) discards the data entirely (the gate output is identical for every stratum regardless of what was observed), and (ii) lets regimes with short horizons be forecast beyond their own h\* whenever other regimes have long ones. Additionally the gate crosses on the **median** drift curve although `h_star_q05` (the conservative envelope) is computed and then ignored.

**Prescription (P1).** Restore worst-case semantics exactly as pre-registered:
- A′: evaluate d(θ) per stratum with φ_g = Σ_r ζ_g(r)·logit⁻¹(α_activity[r,k]) per (stratum, tactic) cell (regime-occupancy-weighted prevalence, not unweighted cell mean), gate each cell on median d ≥ 0.05; the fit-level gate is the **min over cells routed to headline**, not the mean.
- B′ — two distinct tracks, not to be conflated:
  - **Track 1 (restoration, no pre-registration change):** re-implement the pre-registered two-condition rule with its stated semantics — the lower-95 floor on the per-cell I(O) (not a cross-source mean; if a cross-source reduction is unavoidable at the fit level, the pre-registration-consistent reduction is the third-best source, J₍₃₎ semantics mirroring A′), and N_eff·I² ≥ 3.0 with per-stratum N_g (not pooled U) and the Godambe adjustment. This is the minimal fix and restores conformance to the thesis as written.
  - **Track 2 (successor gate, requires model-version bump and pre-registration amendment):** the theorem-consistent aggregate is the additive conditional Fisher information, F_tot(θ) = N_eff · Σ_{s=1}^{S} I_s²/(p_s(1−p_s)), p_s = δ_s + I_s φ; gate on the 2.5% posterior quantile of F_tot ≥ 12 (same 1/12 flat-prior-variance rationale: Var ≥ 1/F, require 1/F ≤ 1/12). This single condition subsumes both FG3 sub-conditions and is monotone in each source's true contribution — a useless source adds ≈ 0 instead of diluting the statistic. It is *recommended* but it is a change of gate, not a restoration; adopting it without a version bump would repeat the C1 pattern.
- D′: compute drift(h) from the per-stratum filtered terminal posterior ζ_T (already available — `terminal_regime_posterior` in Stan), and gate on the **q95 drift envelope's** first crossing (= `h_star_q05`), i.e. h\*_gate = min over posterior envelope, per stratum. Remove the stationary-weighted average entirely.

Any change of an aggregation functional in a pre-registered gate must itself be pre-registered; silent min→mean edits void the audit trail. Add a conformance test asserting the functional form of each gate statistic against the thesis.

### C2. The falsification battery is fail-open (`run_battery`, `falsification.py`, router FG1)

`run_battery` marks any test with missing inputs as `"skip"`, and `"skip"` does not fail the battery. `_assemble_inputs` only ever wires F1, F12, F13, F14. Therefore F3–F11 and F15 are **permanently skipped** in production, the ledger reports `gate_status: "pass"`, and the router's FG1 explicitly treats `"skip"` as passing. Most damaging: **F11 (conditional independence of sources) — the only operational enforcement of assumption (A2) of Theorem A′ and the engine's stated defense against correlated-source / hallucination-cascade failure — has no input assembly anywhere in the pipeline**, and even when invoked with zero pairs returns `"pass"` ("no_pairs"). The Source Independence Ledger exists as configuration but no code path computes the pairwise residual-dependence p-values F11 consumes.

**Prescription (P2).** Invert the default: partition the battery into `REQUIRED = {F1, F11, F12, F13, F14}` and `CONDITIONAL = {F3, F4, F5, F6, F7, F15}` (the latter skippable only with a pre-registered reason code). A required test in state `skip` must route the run to `withhold`. For F11, implement the over-identification test stated in thesis §7.1: for S ≥ 3 (and within each ledger block for S > 3), compare observed joint cell frequencies of (Y_1,…,Y_S) to the fitted product-form law; test statistic G² = 2 Σ_cells n_c log(n_c/ n p̂_c) on the surplus degrees of freedom (2^S − 1) − (2S + 1), referred to χ² with that df (or a posterior-predictive p-value from the fitted model). When S < 3 within a ledger block the test is unavailable and the block-collapse fail-closed branch of the thesis defense (K < 3 ⟹ `non_identified_dependent_sources`) must be enforced mechanically from the ledger, not by quarterly human review alone.

### C3. Unit error: dwell λ passed where the dwell **mean** μ = λ + 1 is required (D′ and B′ kernels)

`D_forecast_horizon.py::stationary_marginal_time` computes π_∞[r] ∝ ν[r]·μ[r] and `forecast_regime_distribution` samples dwell as Poisson(μ−1)+1 — both correctly written in terms of the dwell **mean** μ. But `fit.py` passes `dwell_d = fit.stan_variable("dwell_lambda")`, i.e. the shifted-Poisson **rate** λ, whose mean is μ = λ + 1. Consequences: (i) π_∞ is mis-weighted (∝ νλ instead of ν(λ+1)) — wrong stationary reference for drift(h) and for the D′ averaging weights; (ii) simulated dwells have mean λ instead of λ+1 — every regime is one month too short in the drift simulation; (iii) for any posterior draw with λ < 1, `rng.poisson(λ−1)` is called with a negative rate (exception → gate `"error"`); (iv) the same λ-as-μ substitution propagates into `compute_activity_autocorrelation` and hence into the Godambe N_eff of FG3.

**Prescription (P3).** Define a single conversion at the kernel boundary: μ_r = λ_r + 1 for the shifted-Poisson dwell; pass μ to all renewal functionals; sample dwell as 1 + Poisson(λ). Add a unit test pinning E[D] = λ + 1 against the Stan `shifted_poisson_rng`.

### C4. The h\* kernel forecasts under a different law than the actual forecast: fresh dwell at the origin

The thesis records (Acknowledged limitation #4) that fresh-dwell sampling at the forecast origin was a bug, *corrected* by the stationary renewal (length-biased) draw — and `m01_forward.stan`'s generated quantities indeed implement the correct mixture: D\* ~ (1/(λ+1))·SP(λ) + (λ/(λ+1))·(SP(λ)+1), residual ~ Uniform{1..D\*} (verified: this is the exact size-biased law of 1+Poisson(λ)). But `forecast_regime_distribution` — the kernel that computes drift(h) and therefore h\* — **draws a fresh full dwell at the origin** with no renewal correction. So FG5's boundary is computed on a forecast law that overstates near-horizon persistence relative to the law actually used to emit forecasts: drift(h) is biased low, h\* biased high, the gate anti-conservative. (Inside `compute_activity_autocorrelation` the same fresh-dwell error biases Corr(A_0,A_h) up and N_eff down, which is conservative there — but the inconsistency means FG3 and FG5 disagree about the process law.)

**Prescription (P4).** There must be exactly one forecast law. Either (a) port the stationary renewal correction into `forecast_regime_distribution` (identical mixture as the Stan GQ), or — preferable, eliminating the twin-drift risk — (b) compute drift(h) **directly from the Stan `z_future` posterior draws**: forecast_h[r] = (1/D)Σ_draws 1{z_future[·,h] = r}, then drift(h) = 1 − TV(forecast_h, π_∞)/(TV(forecast_h, π_∞) + TV(forecast_h, ζ_T)). This makes FG5 a diagnostic of the actual emitted forecast rather than of a parallel Python simulator.

### C5. The Dawid–Skene coder layer cannot represent the adversarial LLM failure modes it is meant to guard against

Four compounding facts in `m01_forward.stan` / `coder_calibration.stan`:

1. **Hard better-than-chance constraint.** κ⁺_m, κ⁻_m = 0.5 + 0.5·logit⁻¹(·) ∈ (0.5, 1). A systematically biased or anti-correlated coder (prompt-injection victim, inverted rubric, adversarial drift) is inexpressible; its errors are forcibly re-attributed to the source channel or the latent state. Combined with the informative prior N(1, 0.5) on the raw scale (κ ≈ 0.87 a priori), the model *presumes* a competent pool.
2. **Conditional independence of coders given b.** P(y_{1:M}|b) = Π_m P(y_m|b). Catastrophic consensus — all LLMs misreading the same document the same way (shared base models, shared prompt template, shared retrieved text) — multiplies M agreeing likelihood terms and produces posterior odds growing geometrically in M for what is epistemically **one** observation. This is the precise mechanism of a hallucination cascade, and nothing in the Stan likelihood damps it.
3. **The one mechanism that did damp it was deleted.** The change-log comment in `m01_forward.stan` records that per-item ambiguity random effects were removed for sampling speed, asserting "None of these parameters appear in the theorem gates... so removing them does not affect gate validity." That justification is mathematically false: the shared per-item effect was the only source of conditional dependence between coders given b; removing it changes the posteriors of κ, (ρ, δ), hence I(O), d(θ), and every gate downstream. Gate *formulas* unchanged ≠ gate *inputs* unchanged.
4. **Calibration is not actually propagated.** ARCHITECTURE §6.3 states the gold-calibrated κ posteriors "enter the main fit exactly like human coders," but `m01_forward.stan` re-estimates κ from fixed N(1, 0.5) priors; no artifact from `coder_calibration.stan` parameterizes the main fit. The hard gate "no headline claims for un-calibrated tactics" has no code expression in the fit path.

**Prescription (P5).** In order of force:
- (i) Reinstate conditional dependence given b with a **non-centered** shared item effect (fixing the Neal's-funnel mixing problem rather than deleting the science): y_{u,s,m} | b, η_{u,s} ~ Bernoulli(logit⁻¹(logit(κ-term_m) + η_{u,s})), η_{u,s} = σ_η · η̃_{u,s}, η̃ ~ N(0,1), σ_η ~ N⁺(0, 0.5). Equivalently (cheaper, M marginalizable in closed form): replace the per-(u,s) coder block with a two-component common-mode mixture, P(y_{1:M}|b) = (1−w)·Π_m Bern(y_m|κ-term) + w·[common-mode term], w ~ Beta(1, 9), which caps the log-evidence of unanimous agreement at log(1/w) instead of growing linearly in M.
- (ii) Until (i) lands, apply the design-effect discount **inside the likelihood's information accounting**, not just as a side sensitivity analysis: M_eff = M / (1 + (M−1)·ρ̄), with ρ̄ the within-family inter-coder error correlation estimated on the gold set; equivalently temper each coder block by exponent M_eff/M. The thesis's "one coder per family" count is the ρ̄ = 1 corner of this formula and should be the reported lower bound.
- (iii) Propagate calibration: feed `coder_calibration.stan` posteriors into `m01_forward.stan` as per-coder priors on the raw scale — κ_raw,m ~ N(m̂_m, ŝ_m) moment-matched from the calibration posterior — and make the absence of a calibration artifact for any active coder a hard `withhold` for the affected tactics.
- (iv) Relax the κ > 0.5 floor to a mixture prior admitting a small adversarial component (e.g. π_adv = 0.02 mass on κ ∈ (0, 0.5)), or at minimum monitor gold-set κ̂ < 0.55 as a tripwire that ejects the coder from the pool. Orientation/label anchoring is already carried by the source layer (ρ_s > δ_s by construction) and by gold anchoring, so the floor is not needed for identifiability.

### C6. Prior–gate circularity: priors tuned until gates pass

Two comments in `m01_forward.stan` document the practice: dwell_lambda prior moved from log(3) to log(6) — "*longer prior needed for h_star ≥ 3 gate (D′)*" — and the activity prior tuned until prior-predictive Y-rates pass the F1 band, where the F1 band itself is computed in `fit.py` **from the observed data being fit** (with a width clamp to "prevent false negatives"). The D′ gate exists to detect prior domination; choosing the prior to lengthen h\* is exactly the failure mode the gate guards, executed upstream of the gate. F1 as implemented is a prior-predictive *consistency* check that has been used as a prior *selection* target — double use of the data.

**Prescription (P6).** (a) The dwell prior must be justified from external evidence (cited dwell distributions of interference regimes), pre-registered, and accompanied by a mandatory **prior-sensitivity column** in the route ledger: h\* recomputed under the hyperprior perturbed by ±1 prior SD on log λ; if h\*_gate changes route for any cell, route that cell to `monitor_only`. (b) F1's historical band must come from a frozen reference window disjoint from the fit data, and the prior may not be re-tuned against it post hoc without a model-version bump and SBC re-run (the version bump discipline exists; enforce it on prior edits).

## 2. CALIBRATION RISKS (SBC and gate-statistic estimation)

### S1. SBC ranks are computed from unvalidated, autocorrelated chains
`fit_measurement_layer` runs 2 chains × 200/200 with no divergence, R̂, or ESS screening, and ranks use all 400 raw draws. Talts et al. (2018) require approximately independent draws; autocorrelation alone distorts rank histograms (spurious U-shapes), and including non-converged fits invalidates the calibration claim in both directions. **Prescription:** thin each fit to L′ = min(ESS_bulk) draws before ranking (rank ∈ {0..L′}); discard (and count) worlds with divergences > 0 or R̂ > 1.05; report the discard fraction — if it exceeds a pre-registered ceiling (e.g. 10%), SBC fails on computational grounds irrespective of uniformity.

### S2. The uniformity test stack is statistically misassembled
(i) KS is applied to discrete ranks against a continuous uniform CDF — conservative bias, exact only as draws → ∞; the canonical test for SBC is the χ² on B bins with B | (L′+1). (ii) The χ² statistic and p-value **are computed but do not gate** — only KS enters `failed_parameters`. (iii) Bonferroni across ~50 parameters is the wrong multiplicity direction for a *falsification* instrument: it controls the probability of falsely locking the engine, at the cost of power to detect real likelihood errors. A calibration gate should be sensitive, not specific. **Prescription:** gate on the binned χ² (B = 20, expected ≥ 5 per bin given n_worlds ≥ 100); control multiplicity by Benjamini–Hochberg at α = 0.05 across parameters (report Bonferroni alongside); declare failure if **either** any BH-rejected parameter exists **or** the global statistic Σ_params χ²_p (df = params × (B−1)) rejects.

### S3. Shape-diagnostic taxonomy is mislabeled and unwired
In standard SBC semantics: ∪-shaped ⟹ posterior **under-dispersed** (overconfident — the signature of likelihood double-counting / correlated-coder overcounting); ∩-shaped (center-clustered) ⟹ posterior **over-dispersed**; one-sided skew ⟹ bias. The code comments invert this ("center_clustered — likelihood overcounting"). The labels feed human review; an inverted label sends the engineer to the wrong fix. The EXCESS = 1.35 threshold is also un-pre-registered and lives outside `config.py`. **Prescription:** fix the comment/label mapping; move EXCESS to config as a pre-registered constant; and route `U_shaped` specifically as a *coder-correlation tripwire* (cross-reference C5): a ∪-shape on κ or (ρ, δ) parameters is the expected SBC signature of the deleted ambiguity layer.

### S4. SBC scope: defaults below the identifiability threshold, and a category confusion
The default `HyperPrior(S=2, …)` runs SBC below the S ≥ 3 Kruskal threshold. SBC will (correctly) pass on a non-identified model — Bayesian calibration over the prior predictive holds regardless of identifiability. This is a feature of SBC, not a bug, but the docs repeatedly lean on SBC as evidence for the identifiability conjectures of the full production likelihood (thesis Acknowledged limitation #1). **SBC can never carry that weight.** **Prescription:** run SBC at the production design point (S ≥ 3, L and K as deployed); separately, support the identifiability conjecture with what can test it: simulation studies showing posterior *contraction* of (φ, ρ, δ) as N_g grows (calibration plus contraction is evidence of practical identification; calibration alone is not). State in the model card that SBC validates computational faithfulness, not identification.

### S5. MCSE/ESS machinery under the FDP gate is anti-conservative in two ways
(i) `_bulk_ess_1d` treats concatenated chains as one chain (Geyer pairs, no split-R̂, no rank normalization): if chains disagree, between-chain variance is invisible, ESS is overestimated, MCSE underestimated, and cells that should be ejected by the C-6 floor stay in the family. (ii) The exceedance downward scan accepts the first m with empirical exceedance fraction ≤ α, using the same draws to select and to certify — multiple-look optimism on a Monte-Carlo estimate whose binomial noise is unmodeled. **Prescription:** (i) compute bulk-ESS per chain on rank-normalized split chains (Vehtari et al. 2021) and combine; (ii) certify a prefix only when the upper confidence limit clears α: accept m iff p̂_exc + z_{0.95}·√(p̂_exc(1−p̂_exc)/ESS_draws) ≤ α, with ESS_draws the effective number of joint draws (not raw count).

## 3. THEOREM-BY-THEOREM VERDICTS

**Theorem A′ (identifiability).** Theory: sound as restated (Kruskal application, degenerate-alternative exclusion, swap resolution via ρ > δ all verified line-by-line; the S > 3 triple-marginalization argument is correct). The Stan parameterization ρ = δ + (1−δ)·logit⁻¹(j) does enforce the orientation as claimed. Kernel `A_prime.py` (J₍₃₎, min with class balance) matches the thesis. **The defect is at the call site** (C1a: mean-φ) plus: (ρ, δ) are evaluated at a single plug-in observability O = 0.5 rather than integrated over the unit observability distribution — near the δ_max cap the link is nonlinear and a single grid point can sit on the favorable side. Prescribe evaluating d(θ) on the pre-registered `obs_grid` already defined in `synthetic_generator.stan` and gating on the worst grid point. Doc hygiene: `THEOREM_1_DEEP_DIVE.md` still carries the pre-audit overclaims ("exactly one unique solution", 7-equations-7-unknowns as "completely determined") that the thesis core demoted, and misattributes the latent-class swap fix to the regime ordering constraint (`alpha_activity[:,0]` ordering breaks the L! **regime** symmetry; the A=0/1 class swap is broken by ρ > δ). Realign or mark superseded.

**Theorem B′ (informativeness).** Theory: B′.1 and B′.2 proofs verified; the Schur-complement caveat and N_eq remark are correct and honest. Implementation defects: C1b (mean over sources); N is `U = R·T·K` pooled over **all** strata and tactics, where B′.2's N counts replicates within the cell being gated — prescribe per-stratum (per-cell) N_g; the Godambe correction is wired (good) but evaluated at posterior plug-in medians — acceptable as a labeled approximation, but it inherits the λ/μ bug (C3) and the fresh-dwell bias (C4) through `compute_activity_autocorrelation`. The architecture's open item #6 ("is FG3's N dependence-adjusted in production?") can now be closed: **it is adjusted in `fit.py`'s B′ gate, but `router.py`'s FG3 reads `cell["informativeness_n_eff_i2"]` with default ∞ when the key is absent** — a missing field silently passes condition 2. Prescribe: absent key ⟹ fail (fail-closed on missing evidence), and populate the field from the per-cell computation.

**Theorem C (posterior expected FDP).** Theory: proof verified; the σ(D)-measurability argument is correct and the implementation honors it (expectation gate consumes marginals only — checked). `compute_posterior_fdp_threshold` implements m\* exactly (running mean of 1−p₍ⱼ₎ nondecreasing ⟹ max-feasible prefix; correct). Markov fallback condition q ≤ γα verified algebraically. Index bookkeeping between router and kernel verified consistent. The q-reference logic is robust to arbitrary dependence and to multiplicity across tactics/horizons **by construction** (the bound is on the realized family, whatever its size). Remaining risks are estimation-layer only: S5 (MCSE/ESS, scan optimism) and the global caveat that the guarantee is conditional on posterior validity — which C1/C5 currently undermine upstream. One naming collision to fix in docs: thesis "FG6" = exceedance gate; ARCHITECTURE/router "FG6" = λ-endogeneity width (and the router routes FG6 failures to `monitor_only` while ARCHITECTURE §7.2 says `prior_dominated`). Unify names and routes.

**Theorem D-forecast (horizon).** Theory: the lattice/renewal restatement, the dwell-age counterexample, and the first-crossing redefinition are all correct, and `first_crossing_h_star` implements the corrected form. The Stan GQ's stationary renewal mixture was re-derived in this audit and is **exactly** the size-biased law of the shifted Poisson — correct. Implementation defects: C1c (stationary-averaged, data-blind, median-gated h\*), C3 (λ vs μ), C4 (fresh dwell in the Python kernel). Additional minor items: the drift simulator comment claims "geometric approximation" but samples (shifted) Poisson — stale; `forecast_regime_distribution` uses an unseeded RNG, so h\* is non-reproducible run-to-run — seed it from the run_id; when no crossing occurs h\* defaults to h_max = 18 > max emitted horizon 12, which is harmless for FG5 but should be clamped to the pre-registered "maximal emitted horizon" per the thesis.

**HSMM transition parametrization (Stan).** π_ii = 0 via −∞ masking with row softmax is correct, and the right-censored likelihood was verified: `shifted_poisson_lccdf(d|λ) = P(D ≥ d)` (the off-by-one chain d → d−2 is exactly right for the censoring event), and the (z, d) decomposition of the final segment is a disjoint partition — no double counting. Two parametrization notes: each row of `jump_raw` carries one redundant additive degree of freedom (softmax shift-invariance), softly pinned only by the prior — harmless for validity, but it creates posterior ridges; prescribe `array[L] simplex[L−1]` rows with Dirichlet(1) priors mapped around the diagonal. For L = 2 the embedded chain is deterministic and `jump_raw` is pure prior noise — acceptable, but exclude such parameters from SBC ranking (they are exactly uniform by construction and dilute the multiplicity budget).

**`coder_calibration.stan`.** Structurally sound DS-with-anchors, but: gold labels are treated as error-free (adjudicator error unmodeled — acceptable if pre-registered, state it); a single pooled φ_un across all un-gold items ignores stratum/tactic heterogeneity, biasing κ when prevalence varies (prescribe per-stratum φ_un or hierarchical φ); and the un-gold likelihood inherits the full conditional-independence overcounting of C5 — under correlated LLM coders the un-gold items *inflate* κ confidence precisely where calibration matters. Prescribe: estimate κ from gold-only as the headline calibration, and use un-gold items only under the C5(i) dependence-aware likelihood.

## 5. PRIORITIZED REMEDIATION ORDER (for the Engineering agent)

1. **P2** (fail-open battery → fail-closed; wire F11) — restores the architecture's core promise; no math risk.
